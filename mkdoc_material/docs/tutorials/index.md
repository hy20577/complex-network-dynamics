# Tutorials

Step-by-step Jupyter notebooks covering the ideas behind the library, from basic network theory to inferring networks from data.
Each page shows the notebook with its saved results, and you can download it with the button at the top of the page.

## Network structure

1. **[Introduction to Network Theory](01_introduction_to_network_theory.ipynb)**: graphs, adjacency matrices and basic measures.
2. **[Network Types](02_network_types.ipynb)**: random, small-world and scale-free networks and how they compare.

## Discrete dynamical systems

3. **[Logistic and Circle Maps](03_logistic_and_circle_maps.ipynb)**: the two chaotic maps used throughout the library.
4. **[Coupled Discrete Models](04_coupled_discrete_models.ipynb)**: maps coupled on the 12- and 16-node networks.
5. **[Lyapunov Exponents of Coupled Maps](05_discrete_models_lyapunov.ipynb)**: measuring chaos in coupled logistic and circle maps.

## Continuous dynamical systems

6. **[Numerical Integration of ODEs](06_numerical_integration_odes.ipynb)**: Euler, Runge–Kutta and adaptive methods compared.
7. **[Lorenz System with the Library](07_lorenz_with_the_library.ipynb)**: simulating uncoupled and coupled Lorenz systems with `complex_network_dynamics`.

## Information theory

8. **[Correlation and Mutual Information](08_correlation_and_mutual_information.ipynb)**: Pearson correlation versus mutual information for detecting links.
